ABOUT

About GestureControl

GestureControl is a web-based presentation controller that uses computer vision and hand gesture recognition to provide touchless presentation navigation. The system uses the webcam to detect hand landmarks and converts recognized gestures into presentation commands such as next slide and previous slide.
